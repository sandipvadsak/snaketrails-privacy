Snake Trails – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Snake Trails (package com.avy.snaketrails), published by Avedia ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the game.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. We do not require an account to play.

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, hints, skins and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers that store player data.

To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the game uses the services below. They do not receive your name, email or contacts.

2. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google). They may collect:

  • App interactions, such as levels started and finished, hints and boosters used, ads watched and in-app purchases made
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, memory, the state of the app when it crashed)
  • Device and app identifiers (Firebase installation ID, Android Advertising ID) and approximate location (country)

We use this only to improve the game, balance its difficulty, measure ad revenue and fix bugs. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob and its mediation partners Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Pangle (Bytedance). To serve and measure ads, these providers may collect and process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region)
  • Device model, operating system and language
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks)

Each provider processes this data under its own policy:

  • Google: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Privacy Policy
  • Pangle: Pangle Privacy Policy

Ads in Snake Trails are requested with a maximum content rating of PG.

4. In-app purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (for example No Ads, coin packs and booster packs), processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details. We keep a record of the purchase token on your device so that purchases can be restored; Google processes payments under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Notifications and online content

If you allow it, the game schedules local notifications on your device (for example when a daily reward is ready). They are created on your device and no personal data is sent for them. You can turn them off in your device settings.

The game may download game settings and extra level packs from our public website (sandipvadsak.github.io). These requests contain no personal data; like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives your IP address.

6. Your choices and consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Policy inside the game.

You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. Children

Snake Trails is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not include chat or social features. In-app purchases are optional and protected by Google Play's purchase confirmation.

8. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device; uninstalling the game deletes it. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase under its retention settings (Analytics data up to 14 months). To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, contact us with your request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

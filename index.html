Snake Trails – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Snake Trails (package com.avy.snaketrails), published by Avedia ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the game.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. We do not require an account to play.

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, hints, skins and settings) is saved only on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob and its mediation partners Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Pangle (Bytedance). To serve and measure ads, these providers may collect and process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region)
  • Device model, operating system and language
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks)

Each provider processes this data under its own policy:

  • Google: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Privacy Policy
  • Pangle: Pangle Privacy Policy

Ads in Snake Trails are requested with a maximum content rating of PG.

3. Your choices and consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Policy inside the game.

You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

Snake Trails is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not include chat, social features or in-app purchases.

5. Data security and retention

Because game data stays on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it. We do not keep copies on our servers.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
